Economics gives Gold in Civilization VII an economy, and the computer players use it too.

- Borrow from other civilizations or a bond market, lend to a neighbour who's short, restructure an overdue loan, or repudiate it and pay in credit and goodwill.
- Debt can run below zero. The longer you stay there, the more it costs: unhappy settlements, dearer purchases, units disbanded and buildings shut down. Pay your way out or declare bankruptcy.
- Light Taxation, Heavy Taxation and War Levy as policy cards.
- World prices for resources, trade bills between partners, tariffs, investment and aid.
- A business cycle of booms, slumps and Panics, with crises and responses that depend on your government.
- An economic system for each government, inflation, and old buildings that fade across the ages.
- Single-player. 12 languages.
